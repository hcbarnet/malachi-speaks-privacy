Features

What it does

Everything a caregiver needs, nothing a family has to pay for.

A tap-to-speak board at its core, with the tools a parent, teacher, or speech-language pathologist actually reaches for around it.

Tap-to-speak board

Large, customizable tiles organized into swipeable categories. Add a photo, record a phrase, and it speaks instantly.

Sentence Builder

Chain tiles into a full phrase before speaking it, with a repeat button and on-device next-word suggestions learned from real use.

Suggest Next Word

Type to Speak

A keyboard for someone who can spell what they want to say, speaking in their own voice, with the phrases they save one tap away. Can replace the picture board entirely.

Type to Speak

Quick Actions & Recently Used

Urgent words like "stop" and "help" stay one tap away on every screen; a Recently Used tab surfaces whatever was just said.

Caregiver-gated editing

Adding, editing, and reordering tiles and categories sit behind one gear — and, if you want it, an optional PIN. The board itself stays simple and un-editable for the communicator.

Profiles

One device, a separate board for each person — their own tiles, voice, settings, and usage log, switched behind the caregiver PIN.

Profiles

Word usage reports

Per-word 7 and 30-day trends, all-time counts, and never-used tiles — exportable as a CSV for IEP documentation.

Word Usage

Automatic backups

An on-device rotating backup on a schedule you choose, plus one-tap manual export/import to move a board to a new device.

Backup & Restore

Symbol library search

Search dozens of free picture-symbol libraries at once when adding a tile, with control over which libraries are included — or download every symbol once and search them with no connection at all.

Offline Symbols

Voice you can shape

Pick the device voice, adjust rate and pitch, and set a repeat-tap delay to absorb accidental double-taps.

A real voice on any tile

Record a parent saying “Mom”, a sibling's name, or any word the built-in voice gets wrong. The recording plays instead of the synthetic voice, and never leaves the device.

Record a Voice

Show it big

Put a typed message across the whole screen for someone else to read — a loud room, a drive-through window, a waiting area, or a listener who is hard of hearing.

Search your own board

Type a word to find the tile you want to change, matching both the label and what it says out loud — no scrolling through every category to fix “the juice one”.

English, and Spanish in beta

A board that speaks Spanish, searches for pictures in Spanish, and starts from a Spanish starter board — not an English one to translate by hand. The caregiver screens are translated too, and on a Spanish device the app comes up in Spanish on its own. One device can hold both. The Spanish wording has not yet been checked by a native speaker, and the app says so before you commit to it.

Print a board to paper

A PDF laid out to match the screen, same categories and grid. Batteries die, devices get left at school, and no tablet goes in the pool.

Your board isn't trapped here

Open Board Format (.obf/.obz) import and export — the AAC interoperability standard. Bring a board in from another app, or take yours out. Both formats import automatically.

Open Board Format
