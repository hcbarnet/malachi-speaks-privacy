A voice that
learns their words.

Malachi Speaks is a free augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) app — built one real conversation at a time for a nonverbal communicator. No subscription, no account, no data leaving the device. On Google Play for Android phones and tablets, in English and Spanish.

Why this exists

Most AAC apps ask a family to choose between capable and free.

Malachi Speaks started as a board built for one specific communicator — my brother Malachi. Every feature shipped because he needed it that week, not because a roadmap said so.
