Privacy Policy for Malachi Speaks

Last updated: August 28, 2026

In short: Malachi Speaks does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you or your child. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

Who we are

Malachi Speaks is developed by Hunter Barnett, an independent developer. It is a free, tap-to-speak picture communication board (AAC — Augmentative and Alternative Communication) built to help nonverbal and minimally verbal individuals communicate.

Data we collect

None. Malachi Speaks does not have user accounts, does not use analytics, does not show ads, and does not use any crash-reporting or tracking services. We have no server that stores your information — there is nothing for us to collect.

Where your information lives

Everything you set up in the app — tiles, categories, spoken phrases, custom photos, the caregiver PIN, and your settings — is saved only in local storage on your own device. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted along with it.

Camera and photo access

If you choose to add a custom photo to a tile, the app will ask for permission to use your camera or photo library. Any photo you select is copied into the app's local storage on your device only. It is not transmitted anywhere.

Symbol search (Global Symbols)

When a caregiver searches for a picture to add to a tile, the app sends only the search word you type (for example, "water") to Global Symbols, a free third-party symbol library service, in order to fetch matching images. No personal information, device identifiers, or account information is included in that request. You can review Global Symbols' own privacy practices for how they handle search requests to their service.

If you paste a direct image URL into the app instead of searching, the app fetches that image directly from the address you provided.

Backup and export

The app lets you export a backup of your board to a file. This only happens when you choose to do it, and the file is shared using your device's own share/export screen — the app does not automatically send this file anywhere.

Children's privacy

Malachi Speaks is built for use by and with children as an assistive communication tool, and it is designed with that in mind: there are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no social features, and no data collection of any kind for users of any age, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
