Why this exists

Most AAC apps ask a family to choose between capable and free.

Malachi Speaks started as a board built for one specific communicator — my brother Malachi. Every feature shipped because he needed it that week, not because a roadmap said so.

Real screens, from the app on Malachi's own device.

Word Usage, with real numbers behind it

This is Malachi's own most-tapped list — not a mockup. Every count on this screen came from him actually using the board, tracked per word so a caregiver or SLP can see exactly what's landing and what isn't.

The export behind that share icon

Tapping the icon at the top right of this screen turns this same data into a CSV — label, category, all-time count, 7-day count, 30-day count, last tapped — ready to attach to an IEP update.
